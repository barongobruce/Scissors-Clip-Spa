About The Scissors Clip & Spa

Scissors Clip & Spa — is located at Kahawa Wendani, Nairobi — your one-stop shop for hair, grooming, beauty and spa care. We cut and style men's, ladies' and kids' hair, shape and trim beards, colour and bleach hair, retouch and care for dreadlocks, and pamper you with facials, face and head scrubs, steaming, manicure, pedicure and relaxing head-and-shoulder massages. Whether you walk in for a quick kinyozi visit or stay for the full spa experience, you will always leave looking sharp and feeling your best.
